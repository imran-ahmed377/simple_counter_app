## Price Source Legend

- **Confirmed**: price shown on the matching bestbuy.ca listing. Audio rows were checked Oct 16, 2026; all other rows were checked Oct 11, 2026.
- **Tier est.**: that exact colour's price wasn't displayed. I used Best Buy Canada's price for the same model and lens tier (clear/sun, polarized, or Transitions).
- **Likely**: a listing was found, but its title doesn't exactly match the box.
- **Est.**: Best Buy Canada shows no price for this lens tier. This estimate adds the US gap between polarized and sun lenses to the Canadian sun-lens price.
- **Shelf tag**: taken from the in-store price tag in the photo.
